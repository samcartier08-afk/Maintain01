# SOP-MTR-04: Conveyor Motor Drive Bearing Replacement Protocol

## Scope & Applicability
This standard operating procedure applies to 75kW 4-pole primary parcel conveyor induction motors (Assets: M-201, M-202, M-204) deployed on primary sortation lines.

## Symptoms & Failure Signatures
- **Early Stage**: High-frequency demodulated acceleration rise. Vibration RMS elevates by 30% to 60% above baseline under equivalent stator current load.
- **Advanced Stage**: Inner raceway spalling produces 120Hz-300Hz harmonics. Motor drive-end casing temperature exceeds 80°C.
- **Critical Threshold**: Vibration velocity exceeds 4.5 mm/s ISO limit. Cage fracture imminent within 3 to 7 operating days.

## Required Replacement Parts & Tools
1. **Primary Bearing**: SKF Explorer 6314-2Z/C3 Deep Groove Ball Bearing (Part ID: `BRG-SKF-6314-C3`).
2. **Shaft Seal**: V-Ring NBR V-70A Nitrile Seal (Part ID: `SEAL-VR-70A`).
3. **Synthetic Lubricant**: Mobil Polyrex EM Polyurea Grease (Part ID: `LUBE-POLYREX-EM`).
4. **Hydraulic Puller**: 10-Ton 3-Jaw Bearing Puller Kit (Tool ID: `TOOL-PULL-10T`).
5. **Induction Heater**: SKF TIH 030M Bearing Heater (target uniform heating: 110°C).

## Labor, Crew Skills, & Window Requirements
- **Required Personnel**: 2 Certified Mechanical Technicians (Skill: `VIBRATION_CAT_II`, `RIGGING_MECHANICAL`).
- **Standard Duration**: 3.5 hours for full lockout, decoupling, bearing extraction, hot shrink-fit mounting, laser re-alignment, and vibration baseline verification.
- **Safety Permits**: LOTO Lockout/Tagout Form Electrical LOTO-E02, Hot Work Permit not required if induction heater used.

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Request human supervisor authorization and execute physical Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) on breaker MCC-04B.
2. Confirm zero-energy state via calibrated multimeter.
3. Uncouple drive belts and measure belt tension using sonic frequency gauge.
4. Mount hydraulic puller and remove worn DE bearing. Inspect shaft journal for galling or fretting corrosion.
5. Heat new 6314-C3 bearing to 110°C; slip onto shaft shoulder until seated flush against locating collar. Allow to air cool to ambient.
6. Re-grease cavity to 35% fill volume with Mobil Polyrex EM.
7. Re-align motor shaft using dual-laser alignment tool within 0.05 mm radial/axial tolerance.
8. Remove LOTO locks, perform 15-minute no-load rotation check, followed by full 100% load vibration spectrum recording.
