# SOP-CMP-07: Rotary Screw Compressor Oil Circuit Servicing & Thermostatic Valve Replacement

## Scope & Applicability
Applies to Atlas Copco GA-110 air compressors (C-301, C-302) providing 8.5 bar facility utility air.

## Symptoms & Failure Signatures
- **Overheating / Starvation**: Discharge temperature climbing beyond 98°C while lube oil pressure dips below 3.5 bar.
- **Root Cause**: Thermostatic bypass valve jammed open, cooler core scale obstruction, or oil filter differential high.

## Required Replacement Parts
1. **Thermostatic Valve Insert**: 71°C Wax Element Cartridge (Part ID: `VLV-THERM-71C`).
2. **Oil Filter Element**: 10-micron High-Efficiency Synthetic Filter (Part ID: `FLT-OIL-10U`).
3. **Rotary Screw Lube Oil**: Roto-Inject Fluid 20L Drum (Part ID: `OIL-ROTO-20L`).

## Labor & Timing
- **Duration**: 2.5 hours planned. Safety Permit: Depressurization and Cold LOTO.
