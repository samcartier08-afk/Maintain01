# SOP-PMP-12: Centrifugal Pump Cavitation Mitigation and Impeller Overhaul

## Scope & Applicability
Applies to API 610 multi-stage cooling circuit pumps (P-101, P-102, P-105) serving primary chillers.

## Symptoms & Failure Signatures
- **Cavitation Signature**: Intense broadband acoustic hiss resembling gravel pumping, with casing vibration spikes exceeding 4.2 mm/s RMS.
- **Differential Head Drop**: Discharge pressure drops by 15% to 30% relative to suction pressure and flow throughput.
- **Erosion**: Impeller blade tip thinning leading to hydraulic imbalance and excessive seal deflection.

## Required Replacement Parts & Tools
1. **Impeller Kit**: Bronze-Aluminum Closed Impeller D-280mm (Part ID: `IMP-BA-280`).
2. **Mechanical Seal**: Cartridge Mechanical Seal 55mm SiC/SiC/Viton (Part ID: `SEAL-MECH-55C`).
3. **Casing Gaskets**: Spiral Wound Gasket 316SS with Graphite Fill (Part ID: `GSK-SW-316`).

## Labor & Timing
- **Crew**: 2 Millwrights with `HYDRAULIC_PIPING` and `DYNAMIC_BALANCING`.
- **Downtime Estimate**: 5.0 hours planned; emergency failure repair exceeds 14 hours.
